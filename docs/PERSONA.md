# Persona Scope Lock

## Primary Persona: Tariq Al-Ghamdi
**Role:** Finance & Operations Director at a GCC Industrial & Distribution SME (headquartered in Riyadh with logistics facilities in Dammam and Dubai).

**The One Recurring Problem:**
Tariq must continually justify inventory working capital and bank credit facilities (Murabaha and trade credit lines) to executive boards and commercial banking credit committees. In conventional corporate setups, the supply chain team operates in inventory silos (optimizing order sizes and safety buffers) while the finance team evaluates credit lines and interest expense in isolation. Tariq needs an end-to-end, mathematically verifiable chain that directly links operational decisions (order quantities, lead-time buffer stock, and cash conversion cycle days) to bank financing costs, discounted cash flow valuations, 3-statement balance sheet solvency, and GCC Zakat obligations—without relying on disconnected spreadsheets or opaque black-box tools.

## The 8-Engine Differentiated Core Shortlist
This core chain forms the working operational-to-financial pipeline visually foregrounded across the Mahwar terminal and landing page:

1. **EOQ (`eoq` — Economic Order Quantity):** Computes optimal reorder quantities, balancing setup costs against inventory carrying costs.
2. **CCC (`ccc` — Cash Conversion Cycle):** Calculates Days Inventory Outstanding (DIO), Days Sales Outstanding (DSO), and Days Payable Outstanding (DPO).
3. **WCF (`wc_financing` — Working Capital Financing Cost):** Quantifies recurring interest costs tied up in operational cycles and computes immediate cash savings per day of cycle reduction.
4. **WACC (`wacc` — Weighted Average Cost of Capital):** Derives corporate discount rates incorporating GCC capital structure, equity beta, debt weights, and the 2.5% Zakat base.
5. **DCF (`DCF` — Discounted Cash Flow Valuation):** Connects working capital releases and operational cash improvements directly to enterprise valuation and share price upside.
6. **FS (`FS` — 3-Statement Model):** Embeds operational DIO, DSO, and DPO into 5-year integrated income, balance sheet, and cash flow forecasts under Saudi GAAP and IFRS.
7. **Z-Score (`zscore` — Altman Z-Score):** Monitors working capital liquidity against credit rating distress zones to assure bank credit officers and lenders.
8. **Ratios (`ratios` — Financial & DuPont Analysis):** Evaluates working capital efficiency and decomposes Return on Equity (ROE) into profit margin, asset turnover, and leverage.
