# Brag Plan: Mahwar (محور)

## What is this app?
Mahwar is a bilingual (EN / AR) Bloomberg-style intelligence terminal for GCC capital markets that unites 21 institutional financial modeling engines and 22 supply-chain operations engines with zero server sync and 100% in-browser compute.

## The angle
"The Gulf's institutional command center — running entirely inside your browser tab."
Highlighting the raw contrast between ultra-heavy enterprise sovereign finance (DCF, Sukuk, Altman Z, Tadawul screener) and zero latency client-side execution with stunning bilingual cyber-cartography.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Deep obsidian void cracks open. A glowing emerald GCC cartography mesh pulses to life across Riyadh, Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Doha, Kuwait, Manama, and Muscat, while a terminal prompt types: `MAHWAR // THE GULF RUNS IN YOUR BROWSER`.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Beat 1: The Live 7-Exchange Screener (3.0s – 7.5s):** 38 GCC tickers (Tadawul, DFM, ADX, QSE) stream across a high-density financial matrix with real-time composite score cards and TradingView telemetry.
- **Beat 2: Institutional Valuation & Distress (7.5s – 12.5s):** Rapid switch to the Altman Z-Score & DCF sensitivity matrix — instantaneous Monte Carlo distribution curves render at 60fps with zero server lag.
- **Beat 3: Supply Chain Corridors & Bilingual Flip (12.5s – 16.5s):** Dynamic trade corridor routes trace across the Arabian Gulf; an instantaneous instant switch flips the entire platform into native Arabic typography (Readex Pro + IBM Plex Sans Arabic) in full RTL layout.

## Outro / punchline (16.5s – 20.0s)
The camera zooms back out into the sovereign brand crest of Mahwar.
Final punchline text:
**"43 Engines. 7 Exchanges. Zero Cloud Overhead."**
Subtext: **محور · Mahwar Terminal v7.0**

## User flow worth showing
`Entry` (GCC Cartographic Hub & GO Line) → `Key Action` (Select Tadawul 2222 Aramco → Trigger live DCF & Altman Z valuation) → `Result` (Instantaneous valuation sensitivity matrix & AAOIFI Shariah audit with zero network wait).
